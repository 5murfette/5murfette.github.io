Burrow Brawl

Turn-based worm warfare in a sliced-open landscape. Blasts carve the ground, loose rock and parked cars tumble, and the weather picks sides.

Everything loose is a real physical object. Cars, statues, columns, pianos and boulders fall, tip over and roll, and they hurt worms they hit. Undercut ground breaks away and tumbles. Lightning goes for the highest point and anything metal, and arcs jump between worms standing close together.
